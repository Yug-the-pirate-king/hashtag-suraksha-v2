About the Movement

Building India's Most Cyber-Aware Generation

As India accelerates toward becoming a global digital leader, our next generation must grow not only technologically adept but cyber-aware, resilient, and responsible.

Hashtag Suraksha is a national initiative by CERT-In in collaboration with Cyber Buddha, a technology-enabled ecosystem that embeds digital safety into India's schools through intelligent learning, national competition, and student leadership.

"Cybersecurity is no longer a matter of choice in the current era of rapid digital transformation."

"As India advances towards becoming a global digital leader, it is imperative that our next generation grows not only technologically adept but also cyber-aware, resilient, and responsible. CERT-In and Cyber Buddha are launching a joint awareness campaign #Suraksha, a nationwide movement to embed cybersecurity into the cultural foundations of our schools and communities, making it an integral hygiene part of education and daily life.

At CERT-In, we believe cybersecurity is not merely a technical safeguard but a shared responsibility, a societal imperative, and a strategic national priority. Together, let us build not only a cyber-aware nation, but a cyber-empowered nation."

Dr. Sanjay Bahl Director General, CERT-In & Controller of Certifying Authority

The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is India's national agency for incident response under the IT Act 2000.

CERT-In coordinates cyber security emergency response and works to protect India's critical information infrastructure through national-level programs, awareness campaigns, and technical guidance to citizens, organisations, and institutions.

Cyber Buddha is a technology-first cyber safety organisation dedicated to digital safety, privacy, and responsible online behaviour for students and communities across India.

As the technology and education partner for Hashtag Suraksha, Cyber Buddha develops the CASK platform, Surakshak program infrastructure, and all digital learning resources within the ecosystem, in partnership with CERT-In, MeitY, and Summer Art of Living.

CASK Platform

India's First AI-Powered Cyber Safety Learning Platform

CASK (Cyber Abhyas Sakriya Kshamta) delivers structured cyber safety education through free learning resources and an intelligent AI tutor, built in collaboration with CERT-In, MeitY, Cyber Buddha, and Summer Art of Living.

NEP 2020-aligned. Designed for students in grades 7 to 10, their teachers, and schools looking to integrate digital safety into their curriculum.
