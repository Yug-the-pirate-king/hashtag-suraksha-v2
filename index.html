About the Movement

Building India's Most Cyber-Aware Generation

As India accelerates toward becoming a global digital leader, our next generation must grow not only technologically adept but cyber-aware, resilient, and responsible.

Hashtag Suraksha is a national initiative by CERT-In in collaboration with Cyber Buddha, a technology-enabled ecosystem that embeds digital safety into India's schools through intelligent learning, national competition, and student leadership.

The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology has been designated as the National agency for incident response under Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000. CERT-In operates a 24x7 Help Desk and provides Incident Prevention, Response, and Security Quality Management Services.

As part of its mandate, CERT-In regularly carries out activities for development of cyber security capacities, skill building, awareness and citizen sensitization. CERT-In regularly conducts trainings and workshops for officials across Government, Public and Private sector organizations on focused topics of Cyber Security.

We are pleased to announce the launch of #Suraksha, a national initiative by CERT-In in collaboration with the Cyber Buddha, aimed at enhancing cybersecurity talent and awareness among students.

Dr. Sanjay Bahl Director General, CERT-In & Controller of Certifying Authority

"Cybersecurity is no longer a matter of choice in the current era of rapid digital transformation."

"As India advances towards becoming a global digital leader, it is imperative that our next generation grows not only technologically adept but also cyber-aware, resilient, and responsible. CERT-In and Cyber Buddha are launching a joint awareness campaign #Suraksha, a nationwide movement to embed cybersecurity into the cultural foundations of our schools and communities, making it an integral hygiene part of education and daily life.

At CERT-In, we believe cybersecurity is not merely a technical safeguard but a shared responsibility, a societal imperative, and a strategic national priority. Together, let us build not only a cyber-aware nation, but a cyber-empowered nation."

Cyber Buddha is a premier organisation at the intersection of Cyber Safety, Integrated Security and Data Privacy. Combining deep expertise in technology and law, Cyber Buddha designs and delivers impactful programmes that empower citizens, institutions and businesses to thrive securely in the digital age.

Cyber Buddha has pioneered literature that translates complex cyber and privacy issues into accessible, actionable guidance. Flagship titles include The Good, The Bad & The Cyber Buddha, Why Smart People Get Hacked... (commissioned by the Ministry of Information & Broadcasting) and The New Age of Privacy (commissioned by NIELIT, Nagaland) — reaching audiences from school children to senior executives nationwide.

As the co-creator of #Suraksha, The National Cyber Olympiad, Cyber Buddha brings together government bodies, academia and private partners to build the country's largest cyber awareness movement.

CASK Platform

India's First AI-Powered Cyber Safety Learning Platform

CASK (Cyber Abhyas Sakriya Kshamta) delivers structured cyber safety education through free learning resources and an intelligent AI tutor, built in collaboration with CERT-In, MeitY, Cyber Buddha, and Summer Art of Living.

NEP 2020-aligned. Designed for students in grades 7 to 10, their teachers, and schools looking to integrate digital safety into their curriculum.
