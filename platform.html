Learning Journey

Four Years.
Four Transformations.

Four grade territories. Twenty-four mastery waypoints. One complete cyber-safety journey.

VII
VII
Year 01 of 04 Safety & Kindness
Foundation Level · Ages 12–13 · 10 hrs

You matter. So does everyone around you.

A foundation built on human behaviour first, technology second. Students entering social media, gaming, and group messaging learn personal safety, empathy, and responsible conduct across the physical and digital worlds.

🛡
Certificate Foundation Certificate
Units
01The Digital World You Live In→
02Being Safe — How to Protect Yourself→
03Your Responsibility — Not Being the Problem→
04Your Privacy and Your Identity→
05Being Well and Being Kind→
UNIT 01 The Digital World You Live In
  • What the internet is, and why what you do online is real and lasting
  • Apps and platforms you use — age limits, and why they exist to protect you
  • Your digital life alongside your physical life
UNIT 02 Being Safe — How to Protect Yourself
  • Bullying across the physical and digital continuum
  • Stalking — recognising it and protecting your location
  • Grooming — the warning signs, the stages, and how to seek help at any point
UNIT 03 Your Responsibility — Not Being the Problem
  • How children become perpetrators — peer pressure, mob mentality, and impact
  • The real consequences — at school, socially, and under the law
  • Becoming an upstander, online and off
UNIT 04 Your Privacy and Your Identity
  • What counts as personal information — and what you reveal without realising
  • Password safety and the passphrase method
  • Digital footprints and your future reputation
UNIT 05 Being Well and Being Kind
  • Digital wellbeing — screen time, social comparison, and sleep
  • Misinformation and critical thinking — the SIFT method
  • The golden rule of posting and sharing
Outcomes
Understand that online actions are real, permanent, and affect real people
Recognise bullying, stalking, and grooming and respond safely
Distinguish the bystander from the upstander and act responsibly
Protect personal information and understand their digital footprint
Practise digital wellbeing and apply critical thinking to online content
Key Terms
Digital Footprint Bystander vs Upstander Grooming Two-Factor Authentication Passphrase SIFT Method Personal Information
VIII
VIII
Year 02 of 04 Awareness & Defence
Beginner–Intermediate · Ages 13–14 · 12 hrs

Know the threats. Protect yourself and others.

Bridging the gap between knowing danger exists and understanding how it actually works. Students learn to identify real threats, apply practical defences, and respond safely to the risks that arise in gaming, social media, and everyday digital life.

⚔️
Certificate Defence Certificate
Units
01How Threats Reach You→
02Your Digital Defences — Practical Tools→
03Social Media, Gaming & Relationships→
04Your Rights — Privacy & Cyber-Law→
05Digital Evidence→
UNIT 01 How Threats Reach You
  • Malware — viruses, trojans, ransomware, spyware, and keyloggers
  • How malware reaches you — phishing, fake downloads, malicious links, and found USB drives
  • Reading the attack from the victim's perspective
UNIT 02 Your Digital Defences — Practical Tools
  • Antivirus, device protection, and the importance of updates
  • Recognising safe websites and reading the address bar
  • Password managers, breach-checking, and the 3-2-1 backup rule
UNIT 03 Social Media, Gaming & Relationships
  • Sextortion — a crisis that is never your fault, and how to seek help
  • Cyberstalking and location safety
  • Gaming safety — where many threats begin
UNIT 04 Your Rights — Privacy & Cyber-Law
  • Why data-privacy laws exist and why your data is valuable
  • Key laws every student should know, in plain language
  • Reporting cybercrime — the national portal, helplines, and Childline
UNIT 05 Digital Evidence
  • Why preserving evidence matters
  • How to screenshot correctly, with full context
  • Metadata — the hidden story inside every file
Outcomes
Explain how common threats — malware, phishing — actually reach a device
Apply practical defences: updates, antivirus, safe-browsing, account hygiene
Respond safely to sextortion, cyberstalking, and gaming-based risks
Identify rights under Indian cyber-law and routes to report cybercrime
Preserve digital evidence correctly and understand what metadata reveals
Key Terms
Malware Phishing Ransomware HTTPS Password Manager Sextortion Metadata Data Principal
IX
IX
Year 03 of 04 Understanding & Ethics
Intermediate · Ages 14–15 · 14 hrs

Understand how it works. Use that knowledge responsibly.

A deeper conceptual grade where students learn how networks, AI, and privacy systems actually work. Ethical decisions in this grade are grounded in real technical understanding — not just rules, but the reasoning behind them.

🧠
Certificate Understanding Certificate
Units
01Networks & Systems→
02AI, Deepfakes & the Dark Web→
03Data Privacy — Advanced→
04Digital Forensics→
05Complex Safety Topics→
UNIT 01 Networks & Systems
  • How networks carry your data
  • Common network attacks — what they are, not how to do them
  • Securing your network and the forgotten home router
UNIT 02 AI, Deepfakes & the Dark Web
  • Artificial intelligence as a weapon — the risks
  • Deepfakes — when seeing is no longer believing
  • The dark web — reality without the myth
UNIT 03 Data Privacy — Advanced
  • How companies and data brokers use your data
  • Data-protection rights — a deeper look
  • The terms of service you never read
UNIT 04 Digital Forensics
  • The forensic investigation process
  • What digital evidence looks like
  • Evidence you can preserve right now
UNIT 05 Complex Safety Topics
  • Online radicalisation — how extremist content recruits
  • Complex stalking and harassment scenarios
  • Mental health in the digital age
Outcomes
Describe how networks carry data and how common attacks work conceptually
Evaluate AI-related risks including deepfakes, and reason about the dark web
Analyse how organisations use personal data and exercise data-protection rights
Understand the digital-forensics process and preservable evidence
Engage critically with radicalisation, complex harassment, and mental health
Key Terms
Network Deepfake Dark Web Data Broker Digital Forensics Radicalisation Terms of Service

X
Year 04 of 04 Leadership & Future
Advanced · Ages 15–16 · 14 hrs

Lead the change. Build a safer digital world.

The culminating grade, where students consolidate everything they have learned about security, law, and emerging technology. They leave not just as informed individuals, but as leaders equipped to protect and educate those around them.

👑
Certificate Leadership & Capstone
Units
01How Security Works→
02Incident Response→
03Forensics & Law Enforcement→
04Emerging Technology→
05Digital Leadership→
06Career Pathways & Capstone→
UNIT 01 How Security Works
  • Encryption — the science of secrets
  • Why systems get breached — the human and technical story
  • Defence in depth — how good security is layered
UNIT 02 Incident Response
  • The incident-response process — contain, analyse, recover
  • Responding to specific incidents: hacked account, lost device, and more
  • Documenting and communicating during an active incident
UNIT 03 Forensics & Law Enforcement
  • Working with law enforcement
  • Digital evidence in the courts — admissibility and chain of custody
  • Applied forensics lab — scenario-based investigation
UNIT 04 Emerging Technology
  • Artificial intelligence — the full picture of opportunity and risk
  • The Internet of Things — the hidden attack surface
  • The quantum future and what it means for security
UNIT 05 Digital Leadership
  • Being a cyber-safety advocate and why peer education works
  • Reporting, advocacy, and civic responsibility
  • Designing a school cyber-safety campaign
UNIT 06 Career Pathways & Capstone
  • The cybersecurity landscape and the opportunity ahead
  • Educational pathways into the field
  • Starting your journey now — competitions, platforms, and projects
Outcomes
Explain encryption, breaches, and layered defence-in-depth
Carry out a structured incident response to common security events
Understand how law enforcement and courts handle digital evidence
Assess AI, IoT, and quantum technologies and their security implications
Lead peer education, advocacy, and map pathways into the field
Key Terms
Encryption Defence in Depth Incident Response Admissibility of Evidence IoT Post-Quantum Security Capstone
