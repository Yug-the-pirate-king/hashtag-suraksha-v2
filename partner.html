What is Hashtag Suraksha?

Five verticals.
One mission.

Hashtag Suraksha is India's national youth cyber safety movement — built across five interconnected verticals that address every dimension of the challenge, from AI-powered learning to grassroots ambassadors, national competitions, podcasting, and vernacular content.

We reach students entirely for free. Partners make that possible — and in return get genuine reach, credibility, and a measurable stake in India's cyber-safe future.

See our 5 verticals →
🎓
Education & Curriculum
Structured cyber safety content delivered to students in Grades VI–XII through CASK AI and Surakshak ambassadors.
🤖
Technology at Scale
CASK — our AI cyber safety assistant — gives students real-time threat recognition, reporting tools, and personalised guidance.
🌐
Community & Culture
Slanglish and The Phish & Chip Show make cyber safety speak the language Gen Z actually uses — campus-native, shareable, and culturally rooted.
✅ Free for all students 🎖️ USI-backed credibility 🌍 Pan-India reach

Institutional Patron & Anchor Partner

United Service Institution of India

Est. 1870 · New Delhi · India's Premier Tri-Service Defence Think Tank

The USI is India's oldest defence think tank — advising on national security, emerging technology, and cyber policy at the highest levels of government and the armed forces for over 154 years. As Hashtag Suraksha's institutional patron, USI lends that legacy directly to the mission of protecting India's youth online.

"National security begins with an aware citizenry. Cyber literacy is no longer optional — it is foundational." — United Service Institution of India
🏛️ CETANB — Cyber & AI Policy Centre ›
USI's Centre for Emerging Technology & Atma Nirbhar Bharat drives research on cyber, AI, and strategic technology policy. Hashtag Suraksha's content and programmes are aligned with CETANB's findings and recommendations.
🔐 CCoE — Cyber Centre of Excellence ›
USI's Cyber Centre of Excellence operates nationwide awareness, capability-building, and first-responder programmes. Hashtag Suraksha's Surakshak and Olympiad verticals draw directly on CCoE's expertise and network.
🤝 Institutional Network & Credibility ›
USI partners include CyberPeace Foundation, the NCC Cyber First Responder Programme, and India's leading defence and security institutions — giving Hashtag Suraksha unmatched institutional credibility.
📜 154 Years of National Service ›
Founded in 1870, USI has served India's national security interests across three services — Army, Navy, and Air Force — for over a century and a half. Partnering with Hashtag Suraksha connects your organisation to this legacy.
