Your Role

What Surakshaks Actually Do

🎓

Learn & Get Certified

Complete the Suraksha AI curriculum — 9 modules covering every major cyber safety topic. Earn your Surakshak certification backed by CERT-In.

📣

Run Workshops

Lead awareness sessions in your school and community. Surakshaks have reached over 5 lakh students through peer-led workshops across India.

🤝

Mentor Peers

Guide newer Surakshaks through the program. Build your own network and establish a campus chapter at your school or college.

🏆

Compete Nationally

Represent your school in the Cyber Olympiad — from school level all the way to the national finals with prizes and national recognition.

📱

Create Awareness Content

Make reels, posters, and campaigns that spread cyber safety awareness. Top content gets amplified by CERT-In and MyGov to millions.

🌟

Speak & Lead

Captains and State Surakshaks speak at national conclaves, represent India's student safety movement, and receive CERT-In recognition letters.
