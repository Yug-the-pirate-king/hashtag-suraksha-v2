About the Olympiad

What is NCO?

India's first and only national-level cybersecurity competition designed exclusively for school students. The Olympiad identifies and celebrates India's sharpest digital minds — testing not just knowledge, but the ability to think, respond, and act in real-world cyber scenarios.

Backed by the United Service Institution of India and powered by the CASK AI platform, NCO gives every student — from any school, in any state — a fair shot at national recognition.

Three Stages

How the Olympiad works

Each stage is progressively more challenging — from online school-level screening to an in-person national final in New Delhi.

I
Stage One · School Level
The Online Screening
Open to all registered students. A 60-minute online test covering cyber threat awareness, phishing identification, password security, and digital privacy basics. Top scorers from each school advance.

The State Round
